# std_types::hash_bag/std::merge (a method)

  merges two hash bags by summing their counts

  Parameters:
    self: the base bag
    other: the bag to merge in

  Result:
    the merged bag

  The merged bag contains the union of the keys of both bags. For a
  key present in both bags, the counts are added together. Keys only
  present in one bag keep their count. If the sum of two counts is
  zero, the key is not present in the result. A cloned bag is
  returned; both original bags remain unchanged.

  Topic: Bags

  See also: std_types::hash_bag/std::difference,
            std_types::hash_bag/std::add

  Example:

    $shelves empty_hash_bag
    !shelves("apple") 3
    !shelves("pear") 5
    !shelves("banana") 2
    $deliveries empty_hash_bag
    !deliveries("pear") 4
    !deliveries("mango") 6
    $combined merge(shelves deliveries)
    debug::dump 99 `combined
    debug::dump 99 `shelves
