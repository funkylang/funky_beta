# llama::TOP_K (a unique item)

  the top-k option for llama-server completion requests

  Use as a key in the options of <llama::complete> to limit the number of
  candidate tokens. Only the K tokens with the highest probabilities are
  considered; all other tokens are discarded before sampling occurs. This is a
  hard limit on how many tokens remain in play, regardless of their relative
  probabilities.

  Consider a single selection step where the model assigns these probabilities
  after softmax:

    "the" : 60 percent   (highest)
    "a"   : 25 percent
    "an"  : 10 percent
    "this": 5 percent
    "that": 3 percent
    "its" : 1 percent

  With TOP_K = 2: keep only the two tokens with highest probability:

    - "the" (60 percent)
    - "a" (25 percent)

  Now only these two are considered for sampling. All other tokens, including
  "an" with 10 percent, are discarded even though it's still reasonably likely.

  With TOP_K = 4: keep the four highest-probability tokens:

    - "the" (60)
    - "a"   (25)
    - "an"  (10)
    - "this"(5)

  Now these four are in play. The remaining tokens ("that" and "its") are
  discarded.

  TOP_K is a static filter: it always keeps exactly K tokens, no matter how
  the model's probabilities are distributed. This contrasts with TOP_P or
  MIN_P, which adapt based on relative likelihoods. A value of 1 means greedy
  selection of only the best token; higher values like 50 or 100 allow broader
  exploration.

  When omitted, all tokens with non-zero probability are considered.

  Topic: AI

  See also: llama::complete, llama::TOP_P, llama::MIN_P, llama::TEMPERATURE

  Example:

    <require basic/stdlib>
    <require ai/llama_server>

    <using std>
    <using llama>

    complete! $result "/no_think/ Continue this sentence: The sky is"
      TOP_K = 3
      TEMPERATURE = 1.0
      MAX_TOKENS = 5

    println! result

  Output:

    a beautiful blue, and
