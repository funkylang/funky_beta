# llama::TEMPERATURE (a unique item)

  the temperature option for llama-server completion requests

  Use as a key in the options of <llama::complete> to control how
  randomly tokens are selected. The model always computes raw scores
  for each possible next token; the temperature reshapes those scores
  before the final token is drawn. A value of zero selects the single
  most likely token at every step (fully deterministic). Higher values
  spread the chances more evenly, increasing variety and creativity --
  but also the risk of less relevant output.

  Consider a single selection step. The model assigns raw scores: "the" = 2.0,
  "a" = 1.4, "an" = 0.8. After softmax at temperature 1.0 these become
  probabilities of 54.1 percent for "the", 29.7 percent for "a", and 16.3
  percent for "an".

  - Zero: ignore all scores except the maximum; pick "the" every time.
    No randomness at all.

  - Below 1.0 (e.g. 0.4): concentrate probability on the top token(s).
    Now "the" = 78.6 percent, "a" = 17.5, "an" = 3.9. The leading choice
    becomes much more likely.

  - Above 1.0 (e.g. 2.0): flatten the distribution so weaker tokens
    become competitive. Now "the" = 43.7 percent, "a" = 32.4, "an" = 24.0.
    The model can now pick less obvious continuations with realistic
    frequency.

  Think of temperature as a dial: zero means "always the most likely answer",
  while higher values mean "spread across all plausible answers". Values in
  the range of 0.7 to 1.5 are commonly used for creative generation; values
  near zero are appropriate when you want stable, factual completions.

  Topic: AI

  See also: llama::complete, llama::TOP_K, llama::TOP_P, llama::MIN_P

    <require ai/llama_server>

    <using std>
    <using llama>

    complete! $result "/no_think/ What color is the sky on a clear day?"
      TEMPERATURE = 0
      MAX_TOKENS = 5
