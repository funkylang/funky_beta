# std::fill_trapezoid (a polymorphic function)

  draw a filled trapezoid

  Fill a trapezoid region in a screen object. The trapezoid is defined by its
  top-left, top-right, bottom-left, and bottom-right corners. The left and top
  edges are included in the filled area; the bottom and right edges are not.

  Parameters:
    self: the screen object
    top_left_x, top_left_y: coordinates of the top-left corner
    top_right_x, top_right_y: coordinates of the top-right corner
    bottom_left_x, bottom_left_y: coordinates of the bottom-left corner
    bottom_right_x, bottom_right_y: coordinates of the bottom-right corner

  Result:
